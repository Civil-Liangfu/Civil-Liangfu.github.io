Liangfu GE

Research Assistant Professor in Structural Health Monitoring

Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering The Hong Kong Polytechnic University Hong Kong, China

I’m a Research Assistant Professor at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, specializing in Structural Health Monitoring (SHM) and Smart Cities. My research interests include traffic load identification, structural damage detection, robotics applications in SHM, and digital twinning of civil infrastructure. Before joining PolyU, I was a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Western University, Canada. I received my PhD from Tongji University, including a research visit to the University of Exeter, UK.

01

Traffic Load Identification

Who is on the bridge, where, and how heavy? Combining weigh-in-motion sensors with machine vision to map the traffic load on a whole bridge.

Read more 02

Structural Damage Detection

Finding damage early by combining load and response measurements with machine learning, influence lines, and acoustic signals.

Read more 03

Robotics for Inspection

Ground robots, drones and sonar, combined with deep learning, for automated inspection of structures, including parts under water.

Read more 04

Digital Twins and Physics-Informed Learning

Virtual models of bridges and dams that stay in step with monitoring data, and physics-guided machine learning to predict how structures vibrate.
