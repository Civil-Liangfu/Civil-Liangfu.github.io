Research · Theme 03

Robotics for Inspection

Ground robots, drones and sonar, combined with deep learning, for automated inspection of structures, including parts under water.

Visual inspection is still the main way bridges are checked. But it is slow and costly, and it can be dangerous, especially for parts that are hard to reach or under water. This research uses robots and deep learning to make inspection more automatic.

Main contributions

  • Smart ground robot: a deep-learning-enhanced ground robotic system that inspects structures for damage and maps it automatically (Ge & Sadhu, 2025).
  • Underwater detection with sonar: an advanced deep learning framework for detecting underwater objects with multibeam forward-looking sonar (Ge et al., 2025).
  • Drone-based underwater detection for structural health monitoring (Ge & Sadhu, 2024).
